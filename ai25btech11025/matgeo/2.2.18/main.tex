\textbf{Quetion:}
The unit vector normal to the plane 

$ x + 2y + 3z - 6 = 0 $ 
is:

\textbf{Solution:}
The normal vector to the plane is 
\begin{align}
\vec{n} = \myvec {1 \\ 2 \\ 3} 
\end{align}

The magnitude of $\vec{n}$ is
\begin{align}
\|\vec{n}\| = \sqrt{1^2 + 2^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{14}.
\end{align}

Therefore, the unit normal vectors are
\begin{align}
\frac{\vec{n}}{\|\vec{n}\|}
= \frac{1}{\sqrt{14}}
\myvec {1 \\ 2 \\ 3}
\end{align}
